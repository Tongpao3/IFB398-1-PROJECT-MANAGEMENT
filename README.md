# IFB398-1-PROJECT-MANAGEMENT
### **General Objective**

The key objective of Stretch Goals (Project 2 of 2) is to provide yoga business owners with
a simple and seamless way to establish and manage their business online. The project
aims to develop a functional website builder specifically designed for yoga businesses,
allowing clients to create and manage professional websites without requiring extensive
technical or software development knowledge.

Stretch Goals will simplify the process of building an online presence by providing
features and tools tailored to the needs of yoga businesses. The project will also
investigate the limitations of existing website builders and competitors to identify
opportunities to better address the needs and expectations of yoga business owners.
Our goal is to ensure yoga business owners can focus on running and growing their
business rather than worrying about the technical complexity of building and maintaining
a website.

### **Risks, Requirements & Constraints**
**Project Risks**

_Project delays_
Project delays may occur due to ineffective project management or unexpected changes
to work and university schedules, which may shift priorities away from the project. This
could result in project milestones being delayed by up to one week.

_Loss of project data_
Project data may be lost due to poor project management and version control. Project
data specifically concerns the development of the low and medium fidelity prototypes.
Losing this data will delay the overall progress of the project.

_Pandemic_
In the event of a pandemic, in-person meetings may become restricted. However, as the
project is primarily software-based, meetings and collaboration can be conducted
online, allowing project activities to continue remotely.

_Cyber-attacks_
Cyber-attacks targeting QUT may restrict project progress by limiting the team's access
to university resources, including essential software, systems and computing facilities.

**Project Requirements**

_Secure Cloud_
To maintain the security and privacy of future client data, secure cloud storage must be
utilised to ensure that all client data is securely stored and managed.

**Project Constraints**

_Software_
QUT students have access to a limited range of software subscriptions. If software or
services required for the project are not available through QUT, additional funding may
be required to obtain the necessary resources and ensure the project can be completed
effectively.

_System Complexity_
The flexibility provided to users to customise their websites may increase the complexity
of the system and development requirements. If this complexity becomes difficult to
manage within the project timeframe, the level of customisation may need to be
restricted by introducing predefined templates and design options
